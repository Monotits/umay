How Fenno Works

Speech research has long studied features such as speaking rate, pause patterns, pitch variation and vocal energy. Fenno measures these features on your own recordings and reflects them back to you — as observations about how you spoke, not as a diagnosis of how you feel.

Transcription uses Apple's Speech framework in on-device mode; signal processing measures pace, pauses, energy and pitch. These are combined into a Delivery score built only from how you spoke — the mood of what you talked about never lowers it. After a few recordings, Fenno compares you with your own normal rather than with strangers.

Voice & Words places your tone next to your transcript so you can judge for yourself how a moment came across. The Weekly Mirror gathers your recordings into a short look back at the week, and the Energy and People lenses (beta) show patterns across recordings.

Fenno is developed by umay.dev, with a deep commitment to making voice-based wellness tools that are private, accessible, and genuinely useful for daily self-awareness.
