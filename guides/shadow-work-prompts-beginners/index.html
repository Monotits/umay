Shadow Work Prompts for Beginners: 25 Questions to Start

By Buğra Günay, maker of ShadowART

What shadow work is, how to start safely, and 25 beginner shadow work journal prompts grouped by theme, plus how to keep a private shadow work journal on iPhone with ShadowART.

Shadow work is a journaling practice inspired by Carl Jung's idea of the "shadow": the parts of ourselves we push away because they feel unacceptable, such as anger, envy or neediness. Writing about them honestly can make reactions easier to understand.

Before you start

  • Go slowly: one prompt per session is enough.
  • Write without editing or judging.
  • Stop if it becomes overwhelming. Shadow work is reflection, not therapy; if old trauma surfaces, a therapist is the right support.

Triggers

  1. What annoyed me most this week, and what does it say about what I value?
  2. Which trait in other people irritates me most? Where do I show it too?
  3. When did I overreact recently? What was I protecting?
  4. Who do I envy, and what do they have that I want?
  5. What criticism hurts me most, and why that one?

Childhood and family

  1. What was I praised for as a child? What was I scolded for?
  2. Which emotions were not allowed at home?
  3. What rule from my family do I still follow without questioning?
  4. What did I need as a child that I did not get?
  5. How am I like the parent I least want to be like?

Relationships

  1. Where do I say yes when I mean no?
  2. What do I hide from people close to me?
  3. When do I feel most rejected?
  4. What role do I usually play in conflicts?
  5. What do I expect from others that I do not give myself?

Self-image

  1. What do I pretend to be good at?
  2. What would I be embarrassed for others to know about me?
  3. Which of my habits do I defend the most?
  4. When do I feel like a fraud?
  5. What story about myself am I tired of telling?

Integration

  1. What strength hides inside the trait I dislike most?
  2. What would I do if I were not afraid of judgment?
  3. What would I say to my younger self today?
  4. What am I ready to forgive myself for?
  5. What is one small action that honors what I learned?

Frequently asked questions

What is shadow work?

A reflective practice based on Carl Jung's idea of the shadow, the parts of ourselves we reject or hide. Journaling about them can make our reactions easier to understand.

How do I start shadow work as a beginner?

Pick one prompt, write honestly without editing, and stop if it becomes overwhelming. Short, regular sessions work better than long ones.

Is shadow work safe?

For most people it is a form of journaling. If painful memories or trauma surface, work with a therapist rather than alone.
