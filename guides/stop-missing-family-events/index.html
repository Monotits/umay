How to Stop Missing Family Events: A Calendar Setup for Busy Dads

By Buğra Günay, maker of Cornerman

A simple setup to catch school events, birthdays and clashing appointments before they slip: one shared calendar, consistent event details, and an on-device assistant that flags conflicts.

Most missed family events are not forgotten; they were never in the place you look. A school email, a message in a parent group and a work invite all live in different apps. A few habits make your calendar the single source of truth, and an assistant can then warn you in time.

1. Put everything in one calendar you share

Create a family calendar in iCloud Calendar and share it with your partner. Add school terms, activities, birthdays and appointments there, not in notes or chats. If your work calendar is separate, add it to the iPhone Calendar app so both appear together.

2. Add events the moment you hear about them

The gap between "I'll add it later" and actually adding it is where events get lost. Add it immediately with a clear title, the right date and time, and a location if it has one.

3. Link people to events

Include the child's name in the title, or invite the relevant contacts. That makes it obvious whose event it is and lets an assistant recognize which events involve the people who matter most to you.

4. Check the week ahead on Sunday

Spend five minutes looking at the next seven days with your partner. Look for clashes: a late meeting on the evening of a school play, two pickups at the same time, a birthday with no gift planned.

5. Let an assistant flag clashes for you

Cornerman reads your calendar, contacts and location on your iPhone and points out conflicts and important dates early enough to act, without you building lists or categories. It stays quiet when nothing needs attention. Everything runs on-device; your data is not sent to a server.

6. Keep reminders few and useful

Dozens of alerts teach you to ignore them. Keep alerts for events that need preparation or travel, and let the rest stay in the calendar.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best way to share a family calendar on iPhone?

Create a calendar in iCloud Calendar and share it with family members from the Calendar app, or use Family Sharing, which can create a shared family calendar. Everyone sees and adds events in one place.

Can a family calendar work across iPhone and Android?

Yes, if it is hosted in a service both platforms can open, such as Google Calendar. An iCloud calendar can be shared publicly as read-only to non-Apple users, but editing works best between Apple devices.

How do I get reminded about school events in time?

Add events as soon as you hear about them, with a clear title and time, and set an alert early enough to prepare. Cornerman also looks ahead in your calendar on your iPhone and flags clashes and important dates.
