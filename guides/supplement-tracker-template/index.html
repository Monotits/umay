Free Supplement Tracker Template (and When to Use an App)

By Buğra Günay, maker of SuppLab

A simple supplement tracker template you can copy: what to log each day, an example table, and how to turn a log into a real take/skip experiment with SuppLab on iPhone.

A supplement log is only useful if it records the same things every day. Here is a template you can copy into Notes, a spreadsheet or paper.

What to log each day

  • Date
  • Supplement, dose and time taken (or "skipped")
  • Sleep: hours and quality from 1 to 5
  • Energy or focus during the day, 1 to 5
  • Anything unusual: alcohol, illness, travel, hard training

Make the log answer a question

A log alone rarely proves anything, because you usually take a supplement every day. To learn whether it helps you, alternate take and skip days in a random order and compare them. Read how to test whether a supplement works for you for the method.

When an app is easier

SuppLab for iPhone keeps the log for you, builds a randomized take/skip schedule, can read sleep and HRV from Apple Health if you allow it, and shows a plain-language verdict at the end. There are no accounts; data stays on your iPhone.

Safety: check with a doctor or pharmacist before starting or combining supplements, especially with medication or a health condition.

Frequently asked questions

What should a supplement tracker include?

Date, supplement, dose, time or skipped, plus the outcome you care about such as sleep or energy, and notes on unusual days.

Is there a free supplement tracker app?

SuppLab for iPhone is free to download and tracks supplements and take/skip experiments on your device.

How do I know if a supplement is working from my log?

Compare days you took it with days you skipped, ideally in a random order, rather than judging by how you feel after starting.
