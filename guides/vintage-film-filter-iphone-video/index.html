How to Get a Vintage Film Filter on iPhone Videos

By Buğra Günay, maker of Cinegrade

Give iPhone videos a vintage, faded or retro film look: shoot for it, pick a film-inspired filter, lower contrast, warm the color and add grain with Cinegrade.

The vintage look people want from film filters comes from a few things: softer contrast with lifted blacks, warmer or shifted colors, muted saturation and visible grain. You can build it on your iPhone in a couple of minutes.

1. Shoot with soft light

Window light, overcast days and golden hour suit a vintage look. Avoid blown-out highlights; tap to focus and lower exposure slightly.

2. Pick a film-inspired look

In Cinegrade, import the clip and try warm film-inspired and faded retro looks. Choose the one that suits the scene, then reduce its intensity until skin looks natural.

3. Fade the blacks

Lower contrast so shadows turn soft grey rather than pure black. This is the single biggest part of a vintage feel.

4. Warm and mute the color

Nudge temperature warmer and pull saturation down a little. Old film rarely had today's punchy color.

5. Add grain last

A small amount of grain makes the footage feel analog. Check on a phone screen; too much reads as noise.
