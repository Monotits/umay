How to Tell If Someone Was in Your Room While You Were Away

By Buğra Günay, maker of Pulse Guard

Simple low-tech checks, door and motion sensors, and camera-free monitoring with your Mac: practical ways to know whether someone entered your room or home office.

Whether it is a shared flat, a home office or a teenager's curiosity, the question is the same: did anyone come in while you were out? You can answer it with no equipment at all, with a cheap sensor, or with the Mac you already leave on the desk.

1. Low-tech checks

  • Photograph the room before you leave: chair position, drawer gaps, items on the desk. Compare when you return.
  • Place a small, easily disturbed marker, such as a thread across the inside of a door or a paper strip in a drawer.
  • Check your computer's login history and recently opened files; on a Mac, a locked screen with a password is the first line of defence.

2. Door and motion sensors

A contact sensor on the door or a small motion sensor sends a notification and keeps a log with times. They work well but need hardware, batteries and usually an app or hub.

3. A camera, if you are comfortable with one

An indoor camera gives the clearest answer, but it records video of everyone in the room, which many people do not want in a bedroom or shared space, and footage may be stored in the cloud.

4. Camera-free monitoring with your Mac

A person moving in a room changes the WiFi signal your Mac receives. Pulse Guard calibrates the empty room, watches the WiFi signal strength from the menu bar and alerts you when the change passes your sensitivity threshold. It also lists nearby Bluetooth devices and marks unknown ones, which can hint that a visitor's phone was in range. Everything stays on the Mac, and Pulse Companion on iPhone sends the alert when you are out.

Know the limits

Signal changes do not identify a person, and pets, open doors or a moved router can cause changes too. Treat any single method as a hint, not proof, and combine two if it matters. For a real security concern, use a proper alarm system and contact the police.

Frequently asked questions

How can I tell if someone has been in my room?

Compare a photo taken before you left, use a simple marker on the door or a drawer, check your computer's login history, or use a door sensor or a camera-free monitor such as Pulse Guard on a Mac.

Can I monitor my room without a camera?

Yes. Door and motion sensors, or WiFi-based monitoring on a Mac, detect activity without recording video.

Can my phone tell me if someone enters my room?

Not by itself. A door sensor, a camera, or a Mac running Pulse Guard with Pulse Companion on iPhone can send you a notification.
