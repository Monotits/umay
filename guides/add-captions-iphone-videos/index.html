How to Add Captions to Short Videos on iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Use Brolled's Auto Captions tool to create word-by-word subtitles, style the text and review a short video before exporting for Reels, TikTok or Shorts.

Captions let viewers follow a spoken video when they cannot listen to the audio. Brolled's Auto Captions tool uses on-device speech recognition to create word-by-word subtitles on iPhone. Here is a simple workflow for preparing a short clip.

1. Start with a clip that has clear speech

Choose a video where the spoken message is easy to hear. Watch it once and decide which part you want to share. Overlapping voices, loud background music or unclear words can make an automatic transcript less reliable, so listen carefully when reviewing the output.

2. Select Auto Captions

Open Brolled and choose Auto Captions from the tool list. Add your video and let the tool generate captions from its speech. Review the result before moving on to styling.

Check names, numbers and words that sound similar. Do not assume an automatic transcript is accurate just because it appears in sync. If a mistake cannot be corrected in your current version, fix the source or use another editing step before publishing.

3. Choose a readable style

Use the available color controls to make the text stand out against your clip. Watch both bright and dark parts of the video, since text that works over one background can be difficult to read over another.

The word-by-word reveal follows the speech. Preview at normal speed to check whether you can comfortably read it on a phone, without pausing or holding the screen unusually close.

4. Preview with and without sound

First, play the video with audio to check timing and accuracy. Then mute it and see whether the message still makes sense. Also look at the frame edges: the platform's interface can cover parts of an uploaded video, so check its own preview before sharing.

5. Check your export options

Brolled's free starting allowance includes three exports with a watermark at SD quality. The product page describes Pro plans with HD and watermark-free exports, plus credit options. Review the current in-app or App Store details before choosing a purchase.

Export the clip, open the saved file and check the complete video. Upload it to your chosen platform only after that final review.

Can I use this for Reels, TikTok and Shorts?

Brolled is designed for short-form video workflows. You export a video and share it through the platform you use. Captions can make the content easier to follow, but they do not guarantee reach, engagement or a particular number of views.

Which speech languages are supported?

The app's interface language list is not the same as a guarantee of speech-recognition support. Check the tool in the current version with a short sample in your language before preparing a larger batch of videos.
