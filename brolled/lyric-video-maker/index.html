Make lyric videos on your iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Lyric videos put the words of a song on screen as it plays. Brolled's Lyric Video Maker adds animated lyrics on top of your own video, which is useful for musicians promoting a track, cover artists and fan edits.

Try Lyric Video Maker in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Lyric Video Maker. Choose the tool in Brolled and add your background video.
  2. Add the lyrics. Enter the lines you want to show.
  3. Style and time the text. Choose a style and match each line to the music.
  4. Export. Preview and export the lyric video.

Tips for the format

  • Show the hook or chorus. A 15 to 30 second section is easier to share than a full song.
  • Keep each line short so it can be read in time.
  • Use your own music or music you have rights to.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own background video?

Yes. The lyrics are animated over a video from your library.

Is it good for promoting my own music?

Yes. A short lyric video of your chorus is a simple way to promote a release on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

What format does it export?

A vertical video for short video platforms.
