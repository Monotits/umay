Add fake iPhone notifications to your video

By Buğra Günay, maker of Brolled

A notification that drops in at the wrong moment is a fast setup for a joke or a plot twist. Brolled's Fake Notification tool places iOS-style notification banners over your video, with the sender, message and timing you choose.

Try Fake Notification in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Fake Notification. Choose the Fake Notification tool in Brolled and add your clip.
  2. Write the notification. Set the sender name and the message text.
  3. Time it. Choose when the banner drops in. Right before the reaction works best.
  4. Export. Preview and export the vertical video.

Tips for the format

  • Keep the message short enough to read in under two seconds.
  • Cut to your reaction right after the banner appears.
  • Pair it with Fake Call or a texting story for a longer skit.
  • Use it for clearly fictional comedy. Do not use it to mislead people or impersonate someone.
