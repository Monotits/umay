Add meme sound effects to your videos on iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

A well-timed sound effect turns an ordinary clip into a meme. Brolled's Meme Sound on Video tool includes more than 100 meme sounds, including the vine boom, sad violin and bruh, and lets you drop each one at the exact moment it should land.

Try Meme Sound on Video in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Meme Sound on Video. Choose the tool in Brolled and add your clip.
  2. Find the moment. Scrub to the frame where the reaction should hit.
  3. Pick a sound. Browse the sound library and place the effect at that point.
  4. Export. Preview the timing and export.

Tips for the format

  • Less is more: one or two perfectly timed sounds beat constant effects.
  • Place the sound slightly before the visual reaction; it feels more natural.
  • Combine a sound with a freeze frame or zoom for extra punch.
