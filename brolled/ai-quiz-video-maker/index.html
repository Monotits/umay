Make quiz videos with AI on iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Quiz videos keep people watching because they want to check their answer. Brolled's AI Quiz Video tool generates questions on a topic, lays out A/B/C/D options, runs a countdown and reveals the correct answer, all in a vertical format for short video platforms.

Try AI Quiz Video in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open AI Quiz Video. Choose AI Quiz Video in Brolled.
  2. Pick a topic. Enter a topic and let the tool generate questions and answer options.
  3. Review the questions. Check every question and answer before exporting. AI can make mistakes, and a wrong answer will be the top comment.
  4. Export. Preview the countdown and reveal, then export the video.

Tips for the format

  • Order questions from easy to hard so viewers stay to the end.
  • Niche topics such as a single TV show, a sport or a school subject often outperform general trivia.
  • Put the score challenge in the first second: "Only 5% get all 5 right".

Frequently asked questions

Yes. The tool generates questions and A/B/C/D answers from a topic. Review them before posting.

Review and adjust the generated content before export so the answers are correct for your audience.

What format does it export?

A vertical video for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
