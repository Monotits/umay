Make split screen reaction videos on iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Split screen videos show two clips at once: the original and your reaction, or two things side by side for comparison. Brolled's Split Screen Reaction tool arranges both clips in a single vertical frame so you can make reaction and comparison content directly on iPhone.

Try Split Screen Reaction in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Split Screen Reaction. Choose the tool in Brolled.
  2. Add the two clips. Select the video you are reacting to and your reaction clip.
  3. Arrange the layout. Position the clips in the split layout.
  4. Export. Preview and export the vertical video.

Tips for the format

  • Add something to the original: commentary, expertise or a genuine reaction. Pure re-uploads rarely perform and may break platform rules.
  • Only react to content you are allowed to reuse.
  • Record your reaction in good light with your face clearly visible.

Frequently asked questions

What is a split screen reaction video?

A video that shows two clips in the same frame, usually an original video and a creator reacting to it.

Can I use it for comparisons?

Yes. The side-by-side layout works for before and after or product comparisons.

What format does it export?

A vertical video suited to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
