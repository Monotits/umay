Make Would You Rather videos on iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Would You Rather videos show two choices, give viewers a few seconds to pick, then reveal what most people chose. Brolled's Would You Rather tool creates the layout, countdown timer and percentage reveal for you, so a full question series takes minutes instead of an editing session.

Try Would You Rather in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Would You Rather. Choose the Would You Rather tool in Brolled.
  2. Add your questions. Write two options for each round. Add images if you want each side to be instantly recognisable.
  3. Set the reveal. Enter the percentages to reveal after the countdown.
  4. Export and post. Preview the rounds and export the vertical video.

Tips for the format

  • Make both options genuinely tempting. The format works when viewers argue in the comments.
  • Five to eight rounds is a comfortable length for a short video.
  • Ask viewers to comment their score at the end to drive engagement.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a Would You Rather video be?

Most perform well between 30 and 60 seconds, which fits five to eight rounds with a short countdown each.

Can I use images for the options?

Yes, you can pair each option with an image so it reads quickly on a small screen.

Does it work for YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Brolled exports vertical video for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
