Make Top 5 countdown videos on iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Top 5 videos count down from number five to number one, so viewers stay until the end to see the winner. Brolled's Top 5 Ranking tool takes five clips and builds the countdown layout for you.

Try Top 5 Ranking in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Top 5 Ranking. Choose the tool in Brolled.
  2. Add five clips. Select one clip for each position.
  3. Order and label them. Put them in order from 5 to 1 and add a title for each.
  4. Export. Preview the countdown and export.

Tips for the format

  • Show the ranking list with blanks in the first second so viewers know what they are waiting for.
  • Keep each clip short, three to six seconds, and give number one a little longer.
  • Ask viewers what should have been number one to drive comments.

A vertical countdown video for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
