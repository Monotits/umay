How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Robert B. Weide. Choose the Robert B. Weide tool in Brolled.

Tips for the format

  • Cut in a split second after the awkward moment, before anyone reacts.
  • Keep the setup short. Two to eight seconds before the credit is plenty.
  • Pair it with A Few Moments Later for a two-part joke.
