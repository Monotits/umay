Put a tweet or comment on your video

By Buğra Günay, maker of Brolled

Comment-reaction videos show a viewer's comment or a tweet on screen, then answer it. Brolled's Comment on Video tool overlays a tweet-style comment on your clip, can freeze the frame while it is shown, and reads it aloud with text-to-speech, so the setup is clear even with the sound low.

Try Comment on Video in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Comment on Video. Choose Comment on Video in Brolled and add your clip.
  2. Write the comment. Type the comment or tweet text and the name to show with it.
  3. Freeze and narrate. Freeze the frame where the comment appears and turn on text-to-speech if you want it read aloud.
  4. Export. Preview the timing and export the vertical video.

Tips for the format

  • Answering a real question from your comments is the most natural use; it also tells viewers you read them.
  • Keep the on-screen comment short; trim long comments to the part you are answering.
  • Only show comments you are allowed to share, and remove other people's personal details.

Frequently asked questions

How do I put a tweet on a video?

Use an app that overlays a tweet-style card on your clip. In Brolled's Comment on Video tool, type the text and name, position it over the video and export.

Can the comment be read aloud?

Yes. Comment on Video can read the comment with text-to-speech while the frame is frozen.
