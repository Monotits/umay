Make tier lists on your iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

A tier list ranks items into rows from S (best) to D (worst). Brolled's Tier List Maker lets you build one from photos on your iPhone and exports a clean 9:16 image sized for TikTok, Stories and Pinterest, with no desktop site or cropping needed.

Try Tier List Maker in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Tier List Maker. Choose Tier List Maker in Brolled.
  2. Add your items. Pick photos from your library: foods, games, characters, places or anything you want to rank.
  3. Drag items into tiers. Place each item in S, A, B, C or D.
  4. Export the image. Export a 9:16 image and share it or use it as the base of a video.

Tips for the format

  • Controversial placements get comments. Put one surprising pick in S tier.
  • Use square, well-lit photos so every item is recognisable at a glance.
  • For a video, reveal the tiers one row at a time with your own voiceover.
