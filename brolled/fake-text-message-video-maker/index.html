Make text message story videos on iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Text message story videos tell a story one chat bubble at a time. Brolled has two tools for the format: Texting Story builds a chat conversation from scratch, and Texting Story + Video layers the conversation over any video. Both include typing indicators, message bubbles and read receipts, with iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram and WeChat styles.

Try Texting Story in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Choose the tool. Pick Texting Story for a chat-only video, or Texting Story + Video to place the chat over your own clip.
  2. Set the chat style and names. Choose the messaging style and set the contact names and photos for each side of the conversation.
  3. Write the messages. Add messages in order. Short messages keep the pace fast; reveal the twist in the last few bubbles.
  4. Add voice and export. Optionally add AI voiceover, preview the timing and export a vertical video.

Tips for the format

  • Write the ending first, then work backwards so every message builds toward it.
  • Keep most messages under ten words. Viewers read quickly but will not pause the video.
  • Label comedy or fiction clearly and never imitate a real person's private conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Which chat styles are supported?

Brolled's texting tools include iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram and WeChat-style chat layouts.

Can I put the chat over my own video?

Yes. Texting Story + Video layers the conversation over any clip from your library.

Can the messages be read aloud?

Yes, Texting Story + Video supports AI voiceover so the conversation can be narrated.
