Add "A Few Moments Later" time cards to videos on iPhone

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Time cards like "A Few Moments Later" or "2 Hours Later" are a quick way to skip time for comic effect. Brolled's A Few Moments Later tool inserts cartoon-style title cards between scenes of your video, with the familiar look and narration style viewers recognise instantly.

Try A Few Moments Later in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open A Few Moments Later. Choose the tool in Brolled.
  2. Add your clips. Select the scene before and after the time skip.
  3. Pick the card. Choose the time card and place it between the scenes.
  4. Export. Preview the transition and export.

Tips for the format

  • The joke is the contrast: make the after scene clearly worse, or absurdly better.
  • Longer skips ("6 hours later") work for procrastination and cooking-fail content.
  • Keep the card on screen for about two seconds so it can be read.

Frequently asked questions

What are time cards?

Short title cards such as "A Few Moments Later" shown between scenes to indicate a time skip, popularised by cartoons.

Can I add them between my own clips?

Yes. You choose the scenes and where the card goes.

What does it export?

A vertical video for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
