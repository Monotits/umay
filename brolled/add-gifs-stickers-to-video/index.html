Add GIFs and stickers to your videos on iPhone

By Buğra Günay, maker of Brolled

A reaction GIF or animated sticker at the right moment adds a punchline without editing skills. Brolled's Stickers & GIFs tool lets you search GIPHY stickers and reaction GIFs, place them anywhere on your clip, resize them and set when they appear and disappear.

Try Stickers & GIFs in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Stickers & GIFs. Choose Stickers & GIFs in Brolled and add your clip.
  2. Search and pick. Search GIPHY for a sticker or reaction GIF.
  3. Place and time it. Drag it into position, scale it, and set when it shows.
  4. Export. Preview and export the vertical video.

Tips for the format

  • One well-timed reaction beats five constant stickers.
  • Keep stickers away from faces and captions.
  • Match the sticker's timing to the beat or the line it reacts to.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a GIF to a video on iPhone?

Use an editor with GIF support. In Brolled, open Stickers & GIFs, search GIPHY, place the GIF on your clip, set its timing and export.

Can I control when the sticker appears?

Yes. You can drag, scale and time each sticker or GIF.
