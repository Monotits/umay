Remove a video background on iPhone, no green screen

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

Green screen videos usually need a green backdrop and an editor that can key it out. Brolled's Background Removal tool cuts the person out of your clip with one tap, then lets you drop in a new background such as a solid color, a gradient or a stock image, so you get the green-screen effect from an ordinary video.

Try Background Removal in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Background Removal. Choose Background Removal in Brolled and add your clip.
  2. Cut out the subject. Let the tool separate you from the original background.
  3. Pick a new background. Choose a solid color, a gradient or a stock background.
  4. Preview and export. Check the edges while the clip plays, then export.

Tips for the format

  • Film against a plain, well-lit wall. Clear contrast between you and the background gives cleaner edges.
  • Avoid fast hand movements and loose hair against busy backgrounds; these are the hardest areas to cut out.
  • Use the new background to set the scene, for example a news-style gradient for commentary videos.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a green screen?

No. The tool separates the subject from an ordinary background, so you can film anywhere with decent light.

What backgrounds can I use?

Solid colors, gradients and stock backgrounds are available in the background picker.
