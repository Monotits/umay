Add a fake incoming call screen to your video

By umay.dev, maker of Brolled

A ringing phone is a fast way to set up a skit or a plot twist. Brolled's Fake Call tool places an incoming-call screen over your video, with a custom caller name, photo and ring duration.

Try Fake Call in Brolled — free on the App Store

How to make it with Brolled

  1. Open Fake Call. Choose the Fake Call tool in Brolled and add your clip.
  2. Set the caller. Enter the caller name and choose a photo.
  3. Time the call. Choose when the call appears and how long it rings.
  4. Export. Preview and export the video.

Tips for the format

  • The caller name is the punchline. Make it do the work.
  • Film your reaction to the call separately and cut to it right after the ring.
  • Use it for clearly fictional comedy. Do not use it to mislead people or impersonate someone.
