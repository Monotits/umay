About umay.dev

umay.dev is the independent app studio of Buğra Günay, a solo iOS and macOS developer based in Türkiye. He designs, builds, publishes and supports every app on this site himself.

Who makes these apps

Buğra has been publishing apps on the App Store since 2020. There is no team behind umay.dev: when you email support, the person who answers is the person who wrote the app.

The apps are written natively in Swift and SwiftUI. When a feature can run on the device it does: Fenno analyzes speech on the iPhone, SuppLab and Cornerman have no accounts or servers, and Pulse Guard reads WiFi signals locally on the Mac. Each app links to its own privacy policy because data handling differs from app to app.
